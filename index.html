Other ways to pay less
  • Library reserves. Your campus library may stock course texts for short loans, free.
  • Interlibrary loan. Borrow a copy for the term from another library.
  • Open textbooks. Some courses have a free, licensed version (OpenStax and similar).
  • Older editions. Often a few dollars used, and usually close enough.
