Is your list longer than one screen?
  • iPhone: take a screenshot, tap the preview, choose Full Page, and save it as a PDF. Then upload that PDF here.
  • Android: use your phone’s scrolling screenshot (Capture more). One long image works here.
  • Computer: easiest of all. Select the whole page (Ctrl/Cmd‑A), copy, and use Paste the page text below. That captures everything, free, without leaving your browser.
  • Or take a few overlapping screenshots and drop them all in at once.
